Our Story

From Long Beach To Reno

Son's Hibachi is a family of five who left Long Beach, the only home they'd ever known, to chase a dream in Reno. The truck took two years of planning and plenty of setbacks to build. In 2025 it finally opened, and Reno showed up hungry.

Every day the family runs the window and works the grill themselves. They care about the food, and they care about you while you wait for it. You can taste the LA influence, and the hospitality is all Reno.

You guys are what makes Son's Hibachi.
